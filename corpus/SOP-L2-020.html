1. Purpose

To pass on the state of the line at shift change so that the incoming shift does not repeat work or miss a held batch.

2. Responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
OperatorCarries out the steps in section 4 and records the result
Line supervisorConfirms the record is complete before the shift closes
MaintenanceAttends any call raised under section 5
Quality EngineeringReviews records during internal audit

3. Requirements

ItemWhat to pass on
Running work orderNumber, part, quantity completed, quantity remaining
Held batchesWork order, reason for hold, who is deciding
Open maintenance callsCall number, machine, what was reported
ConsumablesAnything below reorder point
Quality concernsAny trend seen during the shift
SafetyAnything that could injure the incoming shift

4. Procedure

  1. Confirm the station is clear and the work order is the one on the schedule.
  2. Confirm you are working to the current revision of this procedure. If the station copy does not match the document control register, stop and get the current copy.
  3. Carry out the checks in section 3 in the order listed.
  4. Record the result on the station log, with actual readings where a reading is taken, not just a tick.
  5. Where a check fails, apply section 5 before continuing.
  6. Sign the record and hand over any open item at shift change.

5. If a check fails

CodeConditionAction
W-307Condition detected during the checkCorrect the condition, then reset
E-801Reading outside the control bandStop and raise a maintenance call
E-212Device did not prove within the expected timeInvestigate the first-out device
E-237Consumable below reorder pointReplenish and record on the station log

A failed check does not authorise a workaround. Where this procedure and a machine manual disagree, stop and ask the line supervisor; do not choose between them yourself.

6. Related documents

This procedure sits alongside the other procedures for Line 2. Values that are controlled elsewhere are not repeated here: torque values are controlled by SOP-L3-007, soak times by SOP-L2-011, and alarm meanings by the relevant equipment manual. Where this procedure refers to such a value, the controlling document is the authority.

7. Records

Records are retained for 24 months and are available for internal and customer audit on request.
