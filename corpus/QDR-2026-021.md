# QDR-2026-021 - Quality Deviation Report

Report ID: QDR-2026-021
Line: Line 1
Machine: CNC-L1-02
Part: BK-310-C
Supplier: Shakti Forgings
Date: 2026-10-12
Raised by: Quality Engineering, Hosur plant
Status: Open
Classification: Major

## 1. Description

Hard spots in BK-310-C forgings from Shakti Forgings caused rapid insert wear on CNC-L1-02. Tool life on the roughing operation fell from a nominal 180 parts per edge to under 40 parts per edge.

## 2. Detection

Found on CNC-L1-02 by the operator, who reported three consecutive insert changes in one shift. Hardness traverse on a sectioned forging confirmed localised hard spots.

## 3. Immediate containment

Incoming hardness traverse on every BK-310-C lot from Shakti Forgings, and a reduced feed rate on the roughing operation pending closure.

## 4. Quantities

| Measure | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batch size | 800 forgings |
| Affected | 215 forgings |
| Scrapped | 96 forgings |
| Reworked | 119 forgings |

## 5. Supplier action

Shakti Forgings was issued a deviation notice on 2026-10-12. An 8D report is due within 10 working days of a deviation. The supplier has attributed the hard spots to an uneven normalising furnace load.

## 6. Corrective action and closure

Open. Closure requires two conforming lots with hardness traverse evidence and a normalising process change.
