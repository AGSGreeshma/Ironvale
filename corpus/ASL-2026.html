4. Deviation obligations

An 8D report is due within 10 working days of a deviation. This obligation applies to every supplier on this list regardless of status, and is written into each supplier quality agreement.

A supplier that misses the 8D deadline twice in a rolling twelve months is moved to Suspended by the plant head on the recommendation of Quality Engineering.

5. Review

This list is reviewed quarterly, and immediately on any Critical deviation. Status changes are recorded with the date and the approving signature.
