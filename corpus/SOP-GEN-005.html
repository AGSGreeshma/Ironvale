1. Purpose

To define the housekeeping standard expected at every station.

2. Responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
OperatorCarries out the steps in section 4 and records the result
Line supervisorConfirms the record is complete before the shift closes
MaintenanceAttends any call raised under section 5
Quality EngineeringReviews records during internal audit

3. Requirements

ParameterSpecification
FrequencyEvery shift unless stated otherwise
Recorded onStation log, against the work order number
Retention24 months
Authority to deviateLine supervisor, in writing
EscalationLine supervisor, then plant head

4. Procedure

  1. Confirm the station is clear and the work order is the one on the schedule.
  2. Confirm you are working to the current revision of this procedure. If the station copy does not match the document control register, stop and get the current copy.
  3. Carry out the checks in section 3 in the order listed.
  4. Record the result on the station log, with actual readings where a reading is taken, not just a tick.
  5. Where a check fails, apply section 5 before continuing.
  6. Sign the record and hand over any open item at shift change.

5. If a check fails

CodeConditionAction
M-145Condition detected during the checkCorrect the condition, then reset
E-758Reading outside the control bandStop and raise a maintenance call
E-105Device did not prove within the expected timeInvestigate the first-out device
E-237Consumable below reorder pointReplenish and record on the station log

A failed check does not authorise a workaround. Where this procedure and a machine manual disagree, stop and ask the line supervisor; do not choose between them yourself.

6. Related documents

This procedure sits alongside the other procedures for Whole plant. Values that are controlled elsewhere are not repeated here: torque values are controlled by SOP-L3-007, soak times by SOP-L2-011, and alarm meanings by the relevant equipment manual. Where this procedure refers to such a value, the controlling document is the authority.

7. Records

Records are retained for 24 months and are available for internal and customer audit on request.
