# QDR-2026-017 - Quality Deviation Report

Report ID: QDR-2026-017
Line: Line 3
Machine: LKT-L3-01
Part: SL-220-R
Supplier: Tamron Seals
Date: 2026-09-30
Raised by: Quality Engineering, Hosur plant
Status: Closed
Classification: Major

## 1. Description

Elevated leak test failures on Line 3 were traced to a batch of SL-220-R seals from Tamron Seals with an undersize cross-section. The seals sealed well enough to pass the fill stage but relaxed during the measure window.

## 2. Detection

Found at the leak test station LKT-L3-01 when the failure rate rose from a baseline near 1% to 7% across one shift. The leak-free master still read correctly, which pointed at the part rather than the tester.

## 3. Immediate containment

The seal batch was quarantined. All assemblies built with the batch were re-tested, and failures were routed to the rework bench for seal replacement.

## 4. Quantities

| Measure | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batch size | 6,000 seals |
| Affected | 2,400 seals |
| Scrapped | 2,400 seals |
| Reworked | 168 assemblies |

## 5. Supplier action

Tamron Seals returned an 8D report within 10 working days. Root cause was a mould cavity worn beyond its service life.

## 6. Corrective action and closure

Closed on 2026-10-14 after two conforming lots and a mould change verified by first-article inspection.
