# QDR-2026-012 - Quality Deviation Report

Report ID: QDR-2026-012
Line: Line 2
Machine: FUR-L2-01
Part: HX-180-A
Supplier: Shakti Forgings
Date: 2026-09-02
Raised by: Quality Engineering, Hosur plant
Status: Closed
Classification: Minor

## 1. Description

Core hardness on a batch of HX-180-A forgings came in at the low end of specification after heat treatment. Incoming material certificates showed a carbon content at the bottom of the allowed band.

## 2. Detection

Found at the Line 2 post-quench hardness check. Six of twenty test pieces read below the lower control limit but inside specification.

## 3. Immediate containment

The batch was held pending full hardness mapping. Mapping confirmed all pieces were inside specification, and the batch was released.

## 4. Quantities

| Measure | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batch size | 900 pieces |
| Affected | 900 pieces |
| Scrapped | 0 pieces |
| Reworked | 0 pieces |

## 5. Supplier action

Shakti Forgings was asked to tighten the carbon aim point for this part. No 8D was required because the material met specification.

## 6. Corrective action and closure

Closed on 2026-09-16. Soak time was not changed; SOP-L2-011 v3 remains the controlling procedure.
