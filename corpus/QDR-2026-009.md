# QDR-2026-009 - Quality Deviation Report

Report ID: QDR-2026-009
Line: Line 3
Machine: PRS-L3-01
Part: FS-M8-FL
Supplier: Metsun Fasteners
Date: 2026-08-20
Raised by: Quality Engineering, Hosur plant
Status: Closed
Classification: Major

## 1. Description

A batch of M8 flange bolts supplied by Metsun Fasteners was found with inconsistent flange face finish. Bolts from the affected batch seated unevenly on the housing-to-bracket joint, which gave a torque reading within tolerance at a lower actual preload.

## 2. Detection

Found at the Line 3 torque station during a routine torque audit. Three joints in one shift were found loose on re-check although the original tightening record showed 24 Nm.

## 3. Immediate containment

The affected fastener batch was quarantined at the Line 3 point-of-use store. All joints assembled in the preceding 48 hours were re-checked and re-tightened from fully loose per SOP-L3-007.

## 4. Quantities

| Measure | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batch size | 4,000 bolts |
| Affected | 1,150 bolts |
| Scrapped | 1,150 bolts |
| Reworked | 86 assemblies |

## 5. Supplier action

Metsun Fasteners was issued a deviation notice on 2026-08-21 and returned an 8D report within the required 10 working days. Root cause was a worn heading die. The die was replaced and the control plan was updated to include a flange face finish check per lot.

## 6. Corrective action and closure

Closed on 2026-09-05 after three conforming lots. Metsun Fasteners remains Approved on the approved supplier list.
