# QDR-2026-014 - Quality Deviation Report

Report ID: QDR-2026-014
Line: Line 1
Machine: CNC-L1-04
Part: HX-220-B
Supplier: Arcflow Castings
Date: 2026-09-18
Raised by: Quality Engineering, Hosur plant
Status: Open
Classification: Critical

## 1. Description

Cracked HX-220-B housings from Arcflow Castings were found on CNC-L1-04 in September 2026. The cracks run from the as-cast draft face into the bearing bore and are not visible before the roughing pass opens the bore. Metallurgical review attributes the cracks to a shrinkage defect in the casting rather than to machining.

## 2. Detection

Found on CNC-L1-04 during the roughing operation, when the operator noticed chatter and stopped the cycle. Subsequent dye-penetrant check of the work-in-progress found further cracked housings in the same casting lot.

## 3. Immediate containment

Containment: 100% dye-penetrant check of every HX-220-B housing from the affected Arcflow Castings lots before the housing is loaded on a Line 1 machine. The check stays in force until Arcflow Castings returns three conforming lots.

## 4. Quantities

| Measure | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batch size | 1,200 housings |
| Affected | 310 housings |
| Scrapped | 297 housings |
| Reworked | 0 housings |

## 5. Supplier action

Arcflow Castings was issued a deviation notice on 2026-09-18. An 8D report is due within 10 working days of a deviation under SQA-ARC-01. Arcflow Castings has been Conditional on the approved supplier list since 2026-08-15, and this deviation is the second casting-integrity issue in the conditional period.

## 6. Corrective action and closure

Open. Closure requires three conforming lots with dye-penetrant evidence, a validated gating change at Arcflow Castings, and sign-off by the plant head.
